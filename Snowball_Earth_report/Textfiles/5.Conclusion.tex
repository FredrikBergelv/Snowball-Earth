\section{Conclusion}

\begin{verse}
    Some say the world will end in fire, \\
    \hspace{1cm}Some say in ice.\\
    From what I’ve tasted of desire \\
    I hold with those who favor fire.\\
    \hspace{1cm}But if it had to perish twice,\\
    I think I know enough of hate\\
    \hspace{1cm}To say that for destruction ice\\
    Is also great\\
    \hspace{1cm}And would suffice.
\end{verse}
The poem Fire and Ice by \citet{Frost1920}.
